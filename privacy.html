Privacy policy

8 November 2026. This covers the Irie People posting tool, which the maintainers of Irie use to publish Irie People's own posts to Irie People's own accounts on X, Instagram, Threads and Discord through those services' official interfaces.

What it collects

Nothing from anyone. The tool has no users other than Irie People's maintainers. It never reads other people's posts, profiles, messages or follower lists, and it never follows, likes, replies or messages on anyone's behalf.

What it keeps

The sign-in tokens for Irie People's own accounts, in the keychain of the maintainer's own computer, and a list of the posts it made with their public counts (views, likes, replies, reposts), in a file on that computer. These are deleted when the maintainer no longer needs them.

What it shares

Nothing. No analytics, no cookies, no advertising, no sale or transfer of data to anyone.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/irie-people/irie/issues.
